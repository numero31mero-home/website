ABOUT NUMERO31

NUMERO31 is a brand that re-designs Korean imagery onto fabric.

We focus on reinterpreting prints through the lens of everyday sensibility.









The name NUMERO31 represents a point slightly outside a regular numerical order— a coordinate between tradition and modernity, between practicality and experimentation.
Rather than following fast-moving trends, we study patterns and structures that endure over time, recording daily life through clothing and bags. Before being a brand that makes products, NUMERO31 is a laboratory that designs sensibility and structure.

MERO -
The Persona of NUMERO31

MERO is a presence that reflects the world NUMERO31 observes. Standing at the boundary between tradition and modernity, between the everyday and the imagined, MERO observes and records.

The clothing, bags, and spaces of NUMERO31 naturally appear in the moments where MERO lives, moves, and stays.

They are tools that exist in rhythm with MERO’s movement and life. MERO’s jeogori, gat, and pleated bags symbolically draw from Korean elements, yet they are not fixed to any specific era.
This is not an attempt to reproduce the past, but a question about how tradition can be used in the present tense.

Designing the Everyday

Before creating products, NUMERO31 is a laboratory that designs sensibility and structure.

MERO is both the result of this experiment and an ongoing process— a persona that reveals the world NUMERO31 continues to build.

We choose questions over fixed answers, recording them through clothing, bags, patterns, characters, and space.
NUMERO31 quietly proposes another coordinate within today’s everyday life.

Collaboration Introduction

OH MY GAT × NUMERO31

This collaboration brings together oh my gat, a brand that finds form in everyday movement, and NUMERO31, which focuses on lightness and comfort in daily wear. The pleated bag explores pleats as structure rather than decoration. Its form shifts naturally with movement, yet maintains a composed silhouette even when at rest.

Designed to feel complete not only when carried, but also when placed down, picked up, and worn again, the bag becomes part of a continuous everyday scene.

Large Bag

Large Bag

The large bag is designed with a flexible structure that responds naturally to movement and volume. Its silhouette subtly changes each time it is carried, creating a sense of ease and variation.

Suitable for everyday use as well as travel or days that require carrying more items, the bag offers comfort and versatility without feeling heavy or restrictive.

Pouch

The pouch is a compact reinterpretation of the same pleated structure. It can be used on its own or paired with the large bag to organize personal belongings.

Designed to hold everyday essentials in a simple and practical way, the pouch balances structure and lightness for daily use.
